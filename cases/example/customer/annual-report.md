# Morild Bank – Annual Report 2025 (extract)

> **Fictional document.** Morild Bank is an invented bank created for a portfolio demonstration. All names, figures and statements are synthetic.

## Letter from the CEO (extract)

2025 was a good year for Morild Bank. We grew lending to households by 6 per cent and to small and medium-sized businesses by 9 per cent, and our customers again rated us among the most trusted banks in our region.

At the same time, our customers' expectations are changing faster than our systems. More than 85 per cent of all customer contact now happens in the mobile app, and customers expect to see transactions, balances and offers in real time. Today, parts of our account information are still updated in overnight batches, and our advisers spend too much time collecting data from different systems before they can help a customer.

We also see a growing number of partners – accounting platforms, fintechs and the public sector – who want secure access to account data and services through APIs, with the customer's consent. We want to be an attractive partner in this ecosystem, not a bank that is bypassed.

Our regulatory reporting has become more demanding every year. Too much of it still depends on manual work and spreadsheets, which increases both cost and risk.

In 2026 the Board therefore approved *Morild 2030*, a strategy built on three priorities: real-time, data-driven customer service; an open and secure banking platform; and simpler, more automated operations. Modernising our account services and data foundation is the first major step.

## Risk and compliance (extract)

- **ICT and operational resilience.** The bank follows the EU Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) as it applies in Norway. Critical ICT services from third parties must be documented in the register of information, have exit plans and be covered by regular resilience testing.
- **Data quality.** Internal audit noted in 2025 that customer and account data is held in several systems with different definitions, which makes reporting and analysis slower and less reliable. A common data model and clear data ownership is a priority.
- **Use of AI.** The bank uses simple models for fraud alerts and is testing AI for internal knowledge search. All AI use must follow the bank's AI policy: human responsibility for decisions, explainability, documented data use and compliance with the EU AI Act.
- **Information security.** Customer data must be stored and processed within the EU/EEA.

## Outlook (extract)

In 2026–2027 the bank will choose a partner for a new account services and data platform. The goal is real-time account information in all channels by the end of 2028, secure APIs for partners, and automated delivery of data for regulatory reporting. The investment will be phased to keep delivery risk under control and to show value to customers at each step.
