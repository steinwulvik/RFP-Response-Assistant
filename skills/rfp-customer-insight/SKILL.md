# RFP customer insight and bid strategy

Turns what a buyer has published (RFP, annual report, strategy documents) into insight the bid team can use. In a formal RFP the supplier usually cannot hold its own discovery meetings: contact with the buyer's staff is often forbidden and questions go in writing through the tender portal. This skill works within those rules.

Everything produced is an **AI draft**. The bid team decides.

## Communication

- Talk to the user in the language they write in. Write the bid content in the language of the RFP.

## Before you start

1. **Check the documents.** You need the RFP. Ask for the buyer's public material (annual report, strategy, news) and the supplier's capability source (product documentation, capability sheet or answer library) if they are not attached.
2. **Check confidentiality.** If the documents are a real customer's RFP or internal product material, ask whether this environment is approved for it, or whether names should be anonymised first. Do not continue until the user confirms.
3. **Read the competition rules** (procedure, contact rules, deadlines, award criteria). They decide what is allowed.

## Steps

1. **What the buyer says.** 5–7 points on strategy, goals and constraints, each with a source (document and section).
2. **Hypotheses.** 5–8 hypotheses about the buyer's real challenges and where the supplier can create value, written as "We believe … because …", with evidence and source.
3. **Win themes.** 3–5 messages the whole bid should carry. Each one: the customer need it answers (with source), the proof from the capability source (with reference), and the weak spot the bid must handle honestly.
4. **Stakeholders.** For each role the buyer names: what they care about, and where in the bid it must be answered (RFP section or award criterion).
5. **Priorities.** 4–6 opportunities scored 1–5 on **value** to the buyer, the buyer's **readiness**, and realistic **pace** of delivery, with one sentence of reasoning per score.
6. **Clarification questions** for the tender portal. Ask only about what is unclear, contradictory or affects solution or price. Questions are usually shared with all suppliers, so never reveal the supplier's solution, strengths or strategy.
7. **Solution workshop preparation** (only if the procedure has negotiations or workshops): questions to explore and a time-boxed agenda.

## Rules

- Use only the documents. Every claim about the buyer has a source; every claim about the supplier has a reference to the capability source.
- Do not invent numbers, names or facts. If something is missing, say what is missing and who could provide it.
- Plain, concrete language. No marketing words.
- Mark the result as **AI draft – owner: sales manager / bid manager** and list what the team must check.

## Output

- A structured reply with one section per step. Use tables for stakeholders and priorities.
- If the user wants to keep or share it, make it a document.
- End with the open questions the bid team must answer before the bid strategy is agreed.
